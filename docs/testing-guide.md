# Testing guide

Thank you for testing Reader. Use it the way you would normally read, and tell us what gets in your way.

## What to try

### Getting books

- Add a book from your device: an EPUB, and a DAISY book if you have one.
- Search Project Gutenberg in **Online libraries**, download a book and open it.
- Find a book again in **My books**, and remove one you no longer need.

### Reading

- Try each reading mode: Text, Audio, and Text and audio (Both on the phone).
- Press Play, pause, and change the speed.
- Move by phrase, heading and page, use Go to page, and choose chapters from the contents.
- Read for a while with your screen reader alone (TalkBack, NVDA or JAWS). Close the book, open it again, and check that you come back to the same paragraph.

### Everyday use

- Does every button and control have a clear name when your screen reader reads it?
- Is anything slow, confusing or hard to reach?
- Does anything stop working after a while, or after switching apps?

## Reporting what you find

**[Report a problem](https://github.com/anubhav9001/accessible-reader/issues/new?template=tester-feedback.yml)** opens the Tester feedback form. You can also get there from the repository's **Issues** tab: choose **New issue**, then **Tester feedback**. You need a free GitHub account. The form asks for:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Reader version | 0.3.0 |
| Device | Android phone, or Windows PC |
| Device model and system version | Samsung Galaxy S25, Android 16 |
| Screen reader | TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, other, or no screen reader |
| Book | Gitanjali, EPUB |
| What you did | The steps, one by one |
| What happened | What you heard or saw |
| What you expected instead | What should have happened |

For something Reader doesn't do yet, **[suggest an idea](https://github.com/anubhav9001/accessible-reader/issues/new?template=feature-idea.yml)** instead.

Before reporting, check the [known issues](releases/0.3.0.md#known-issues) in the release notes; it may already be listed.
