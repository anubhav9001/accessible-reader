# Frequently asked questions

## Which books can Reader open?

EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 (including EPUB with synchronised narration), DAISY 3 and DAISY 2.02. DAISY books can be zipped; on Windows an unzipped DAISY folder works too, by opening its `ncc.html` or `.opf` file. Books with copy protection (DRM) can't be opened.

## Can I get books from Sugamya Pustakalaya or Bookshare?

Not from inside Reader yet. We are waiting for access from DAISY Forum of India and Bookshare. Until then, download books on the Sugamya Pustakalaya website and open them in Reader, or use Project Gutenberg from **Online libraries**.

## Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It is planned.

## Why does my phone or PC warn me when I install Reader?

Tester builds aren't in the Play Store and aren't signed with a Windows publisher certificate yet, so Android and Windows don't recognise them. See the [Android](android-guide.md#install) and [Windows](windows-guide.md#install) guides for the steps.

## Does Reader remember where I stopped?

Yes, for each book, down to the paragraph, in every reading mode. That includes where TalkBack, NVDA or JAWS read up to on their own.

## Sometimes I hear a line or two again when I come back. Why?

When Reader can't tell exactly which paragraph your screen reader is on, it picks the first paragraph still on screen. You may hear a little again, but it never skips text you haven't heard.

## The phone's voice doesn't speak when I press Play.

- If you use Samsung's **Dual App**, open the normal Reader icon instead.
- Check that a text-to-speech engine is set up: **Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output**.
- If the voice still can't start, Reader says so. Please report it with the [Tester feedback form](testing-guide.md#reporting-what-you-find).

## Can I keep listening with the screen locked?

Not yet on the phone; reading stops when the screen locks or you switch apps. It's planned. On Windows, reading carries on when the window is minimised.

## Can I change the text size or colours?

Not yet inside Reader. On Windows, Ctrl+Plus and Ctrl+Minus zoom the whole window, and Windows high-contrast themes are followed.

## How do I update to a new tester build?

Install the new file over the old one. If Android refuses, uninstall the old Reader first; its books and saved places are then lost.
