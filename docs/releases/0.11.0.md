# Reader 0.11.0 (Book information, Where am I, books by format, and fixes for your reports)

Released 23 November 2026 for Android phones and Windows PCs.

This version adds **Book information** and a **Where am I** window, lets you **browse Sugamya Pustakalaya by format**, and fixes problems you reported on 0.10.3: a Sugamya download that failed, **settings lost after installing an update**, **maths skipped when reading aloud**, and **list items read three times by TalkBack**. **Please try them and tell us what works and what doesn't.**

## Get the app

Both files are in the Saksham OneDrive folder **Reader tester builds / 0.11.0**, and in the DAISY folder **Reader experimental app / 0.11.0**.

| Device | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | `Reader-0.11.0.apk` (56 MB, build 25) | Android 7 or later |
| Windows PC | `Reader-Setup-0.11.0.exe` (116 MB) | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

Both install over 0.10.3 or any earlier tester build and keep your books, saved places, bookmarks and Sugamya sign-in. `SHA256.txt` in the same folder lets you check the files arrived intact.

To see which version you have: on Windows, **Help → About Reader**; on Android, the **Help** screen.

## New: Book information

Everything the book says about itself, in one place: title, author, narrator, publisher, date, subject, description, rights and identifier; its format, how long its narration is, how many sections, contents entries and printed pages it has; the accessibility it declares; and every other metadata entry.

- **Windows:** **File → Book information**, or **Ctrl+Shift+I**. Each part is a heading with a table, so you can move by heading and read the table row by row. Escape closes it.
- **Android:** **More reading options → Book information**. Each part is a heading; each row is read as one item, for example "Author: DAISY Consortium".

## New: Where am I opens a window

Where am I now shows everything about your place, not just a spoken line:

- **Your place:** the contents entry and heading, the section (for example "4 of 5"), the page out of all pages, how far through the book you are, and for audio books the time and time left (also at your reading speed).
- **Reading:** the mode, speed, playing or paused, and how many bookmarks the book has.
- **Reading time:** how long you have read the book this time and in all, how many times you have opened it, and when you first opened it. Time counts while the book is open and you are reading or listening.

On Windows, press **Ctrl+I** (Escape closes it). In screen reader mode, **=** still says the percentage and page without opening a window. On Android, **More reading options → Where am I?**.

**Please check:** do Book information and Where am I tell you what you want to know? Is anything missing, or hard to read with your screen reader?

## New: Sugamya Pustakalaya books by format

The Sugamya start page has **Books by format**, with the same formats as the website's Advanced search: DAISY audio, EPUB text, Structured EPUB, DAISY text, DAISY text and audio, EPUB audio, EPUB text and audio, and the two braille formats (listed, but Reader can't open braille files). Choose one to list its books, 20 at a time, each with its language.

Searching by **language** is not available yet: the library hasn't opened it to Reader. We have asked them.

## Changed: Sugamya downloads

- **Every text-only format downloads straight away.** Sugamya Pustakalaya confirmed that text-only formats (EPUB Text Only, Structured EPUB, DAISY Text Only and the others) are ready at once, so Reader downloads them as soon as you ask, and asks again by itself when a link has expired. Formats with audio still take time, sometimes hours.
- **Choosing a request that is still being prepared checks it again.** In My download requests, choose a book that is being prepared and Reader asks the library for its status at that moment: if it has become ready, it downloads; if not, Reader says the library's status.
- **Fixed: a download that failed with "net::ERR_RESPONSE_HEADERS_MULTIPLE_CONTENT_DISPOSITION"** (Windows), for example Social Background of Indian Nationalism. The library sometimes names the file twice; Reader now downloads it anyway.
- **Forgot password** and **Not a member yet?** open the right pages of the Sugamya Pustakalaya website, not its home page.

## Fixed

- **Settings undone after installing an update** (Windows). If Reader was open when you installed a new version, recent changes to your settings, reading place or bookmarks could be lost. Reader now saves them to disk within half a second of each change.
- **Maths on a line of its own was skipped when reading aloud** (Android and Windows). An equation that stands alone is now read, as "Equation: …", and Next and Previous phrase stop on it.
- **TalkBack read each list item three times** in screen reader mode (Android): the item, then its text, then the bullet. Each item is now one stop. Numbered lists keep their numbers in the text.

**Please check:** with TalkBack in screen reader mode, swipe through a list. Does it stop once on each item? In a book with maths, does reading aloud read every equation?

## Known limits

- **Quick navigation keys in Text and Text and audio modes:** a tester reported that NVDA's and Narrator's H, K, L and other keys no longer work when screen reader mode is off. We could not make it happen with NVDA on 0.10.3. If it happens to you, please tell us the book, the mode, the keys you pressed and what your screen reader said.
- **Narrator** still doesn't hear Reader's other messages while you're in the book text in screen reader mode (for example "No next heading"). With NVDA they are spoken.
- **Sugamya Pustakalaya:** searching by language is not available; one book (24008) is still refused by the library; and some books' titles aren't found by the title search. We have sent these to the library.
- Reading aloud stops when the phone screen locks or you leave the app (Android).
- Display settings (font, spacing, colours) are not here yet.
