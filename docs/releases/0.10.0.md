# Reader 0.10.0 (bookmarks, Go back, Sugamya Pustakalaya, maths)

Released 20 November 2026 for Android phones and Windows PCs.

This version starts closing the gaps between Reader and DAISY's [Reading Apps User Requirements](https://daisy.github.io/reading-apps-ux-reqs/requirements/published/FINAL-20251031/). It adds **bookmarks** and **Go back**, signing in to **Sugamya Pustakalaya** to ask for and download books, and **maths read in words**. **Please try each one and tell us what works and what doesn't.**

## Get the app

Both files are in the Saksham OneDrive folder **Reader tester builds / 0.10.0**, and in the DAISY folder **Reader experimental app / 0.10.0**.

| Device | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | `Reader-0.10.0.apk` (build 21) | Android 7 or later |
| Windows PC | `Reader-Setup-0.10.0.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

Both install over 0.9.0 or any earlier tester build and keep your books and saved places. `SHA256.txt` in the same folder lets you check the files arrived intact.

To see which version you have: on Windows, **Help → About Reader**; on Android, the **Help** screen.

## New: Go back

After you follow a link or a footnote, or jump with the contents, Go to page or a bookmark, Go back takes you back to where you were. Go forward takes you to the jump again.

- **Windows:** **Backspace** to go back, **Shift+Backspace** to go forward. Works in the book text (also in screen reader mode) and in the contents. Also in the **Navigate** menu.
- **Android:** a **Go back to where you were** button appears at the top after a jump. **Go back** and **Go forward** are also in the **More reading options** menu.

Footnotes now work properly on Windows: following one moves your place to the note, and Go back returns to the sentence you were reading.

**Please check:** follow a footnote or a link, then go back. Does your screen reader read the place you came from? Try it after choosing a chapter from the contents too.

## New: bookmarks

- **Add bookmark:** **Ctrl+D** on Windows; **More reading options → Add bookmark** on Android. The name is filled in with the start of the paragraph; keep it or type your own, then press Enter (or Add).
- **Bookmarks:** **Ctrl+Shift+B** on Windows; **More reading options → Bookmarks** on Android. They are listed in book order with where each one is (heading, page).
  - Windows: **Enter** goes to one, **F2** renames it, **Delete** removes it.
  - Android: tap one to go there; its options button has **Rename** and **Delete**.
- Bookmarks are kept for each book, also after closing Reader. In screen reader mode on Windows a bookmark keeps your exact place in the line.

**Please check:** add a few bookmarks in a long book, close Reader, open it again and go to each one. Are the suggested names useful? Is anything hard to do with your screen reader?

## New: Sugamya Pustakalaya account

In **Online libraries**, choose Sugamya Pustakalaya:

- **Sign in** with your Sugamya user ID or email and password. Reader keeps the password protected on your device (Windows data protection; the Android Keystore) and sends it only to sugamyapustakalaya.org.in. It never goes in the app log.
- **Ask for this book** on a book in a DAISY or EPUB format.
- **My download requests** lists every request; a ready book downloads into My books and opens.
- **Popular books** lists books read most in the past year.
- **Forgot password** and **Join** open the library's website.

**Please check:** sign in, ask for a book, and download it once it is ready. If the library refuses a request, does Reader tell you clearly why?

## New: maths

Equations (MathML) in EPUB and DAISY 3 books are read in words, in English or Hindi to match the book, for example "x equals the fraction with numerator negative b plus or minus …".

- **Windows screen reader mode:** **M** and **Shift+M** move between equations; an equation on its own line starts with "Equation:". **Enter** on an equation opens it on its own, with its words and the equation as drawn (for NVDA with MathCAT, or JAWS).
- **Android** screen reader mode and **read aloud** on both apps speak the words too.
- The sample book `sample-math.epub` has equations to try.

**Please check:** do the equations make sense when read? With Narrator, is anything silent?

## Known limits

- **Not yet tested with screen readers by us:** Go back and bookmarks were tested with automatic checks on Windows only. On Android they have not been tried with TalkBack yet. Please tell us exactly what you hear.
- **Go back** goes back only within the book you have open; it is cleared when you close the book.
- **Sugamya Pustakalaya:** some EPUB books can't be downloaded yet; the library is looking into it. Searching by language is not available yet.
- **Maths:** some chemistry is read oddly ("H sub 2 of O"). That comes from the speech engine.
- Reading aloud stops when the phone screen locks or you leave the app (Android).
- Display settings (font, spacing, colours) are not here yet; they are next.
