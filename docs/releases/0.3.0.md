# Reader 0.3.0 (first tester build)

Released 2 November 2026 for Android phones and Windows PCs. "Reader" is a working name.

## Get the app

Both files are in the Saksham OneDrive folder **Reader tester builds / 0.3.0**, shared with testers.

| Device | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | `Reader-0.3.0.apk` (55 MB, build 11) | Android 7 or later |
| Windows PC | `Reader-Setup-0.3.0.exe` (116 MB) | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

### Android

1. Download the file on your phone and open it.
2. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
3. If Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, choose **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
4. Open **Reader**. The version shows at the bottom of My books.

### Windows

1. Run the file.
2. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. It installs for your account only (no administrator rights) and adds Reader to the Start menu and desktop.
4. Press **F1** in Reader for every command and shortcut.

The warnings appear because tester builds are not in the Play Store or signed with a Windows publisher certificate yet.

## What you can do

- **Read DAISY and EPUB books:** DAISY 2.02, DAISY 3, and EPUB 2 and 3, including EPUB with synchronised narration (media overlays). On Windows you can also open an unzipped DAISY folder.
- **Three reading modes:** Text only, Audio only, and Text and audio, where each phrase is highlighted as it is read. Books without recorded narration are read by the device's own voice.
- **Move through a book:** by phrase, by heading (choose which heading levels to stop at), by printed page, with Go to page, and from the table of contents.
- **Reading speed** from half speed to three times normal.
- **Your place is remembered, down to the paragraph,** including where TalkBack, NVDA or JAWS read up to on their own. When you open the book again, your screen reader starts there, and Play carries on from there.
- **My books:** every book you have added, most recently read first (Ctrl+B on Windows, where it also opens when the app starts).
- **Free books from Project Gutenberg:** search or browse, then download and open in one step (Online libraries; Ctrl+L on Windows).
- **Built for screen readers first:** tested with TalkBack on Android and NVDA browse mode on Windows. Every Windows command has a keyboard shortcut, listed under Help (F1).

## Not in this build yet

- Sugamya Pustakalaya and Bookshare are not connected yet; we are waiting for access from DAISY Forum of India and Bookshare.
- No iPhone or iPad version yet.
- On the phone, reading stops when the screen locks or you switch apps. Background listening and lock-screen controls are planned.
- No display settings (text size, colours, font). On Windows, Ctrl+Plus and Ctrl+Minus zoom the whole window.
- No bookmarks, highlights or notes.
- Books with copy protection (DRM) can't be opened.
- No automatic updates; each tester build is sent as a new link.

## Known issues

- The phone's reading voice may not start in a Samsung "Dual App" copy, which has no speech engine. Use the normal Reader icon. If the voice can't start, the app says so; check Text-to-speech in the phone's accessibility settings.
- Following your screen reader can land a few lines early: when the app can only tell your place from scrolling, it picks the first paragraph still on screen. It never skips ahead.
- On the phone, TalkBack can talk over the app's voice if you touch the screen while it reads aloud. Pause first to explore.
- On Windows, stepping by phrase or heading moves the screen reader into the book text. Shift+Tab or the menu bar (Alt) gets you back to the toolbar.
- Project Gutenberg is sometimes busy. The app retries; if it still fails, choose Try again after a minute.
- Large audio books can take a few seconds to open the first time.
- If Android refuses to install over an older Reader, uninstall the old one first (its books and saved places are lost).
