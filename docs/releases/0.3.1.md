# Reader 0.3.1 (Windows fix)

Released 4 November 2026 for Windows only. The Android app stays at [0.3.0](0.3.0.md).

## Get the app

`Reader-Setup-0.3.1.exe` (116 MB) is in the Saksham OneDrive folder **Reader tester builds / 0.3.1**. Run it to update: it installs over 0.3.0 and keeps your books and saved places.

## Fixed

- **Your place is kept when you quit right after reading.** If you read with NVDA or JAWS and then closed Reader within a second or so, the book reopened at the start of the chapter. Reader now saves your place the moment you close it or open another book, and at least once a second while your screen reader reads on.

Everything else is as in [0.3.0](0.3.0.md), including what's [not in this build yet](0.3.0.md#not-in-this-build-yet) and the [known issues](0.3.0.md#known-issues).
