# Reader 0.4.0 (screen reader mode)

Released 4 November 2026 for Android phones and Windows PCs.

## Get the app

Both files are in the Saksham OneDrive folder **Reader tester builds / 0.4.0**.

| Device | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone | `Reader-0.4.0.apk` (55 MB, build 13) | Android 7 or later |
| Windows PC | `Reader-Setup-0.4.0.exe` (116 MB) | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

Both install over 0.3.0 or 0.3.1 and keep your books and saved places. For first-time installs, see the [Android guide](../android-guide.md#install) and the [Windows guide](../windows-guide.md#install).

## New: screen reader mode

Inspired by the Paperback reader: the book is shown as plain text, read with your screen reader's own cursor or gestures, and Reader remembers exactly where you stopped, even after opening another book or closing the app.

### On Android

- Choose **Screen reader** at the top of the reader, next to Text, Audio and Both.
- The whole book becomes one continuous page of text: no pictures (their descriptions are shown instead) and no styling, but headings, lists, tables and links are kept.
- Read straight through from chapter to chapter with TalkBack, or move by heading, link, line or paragraph with TalkBack's reading controls.
- A blue outline marks where you are. When you open the book again, TalkBack starts at that paragraph, and Play reads aloud from there.
- See the [Android guide](../android-guide.md#screen-reader-mode).

### On Windows

- Turn it on with **Ctrl+Shift+R**, or **View → Screen reader mode**.
- The whole book is shown in one read-only text box. Read with the arrow keys and say all of NVDA, JAWS or Narrator.
- Single keys move through the book's structure, with Shift going back: **H** headings, **1** to **6** headings at a level, **P** pages, **K** links (Enter follows), **T** tables, **L** lists, **I** list items, **G** images, **F** figures, **S** separators, **]** and **[** sections, and **=** says how far through the book you are.
- Play, the table of contents and the heading commands all start from, and move, the text cursor.
- See the [Windows guide](../windows-guide.md#screen-reader-mode) and [keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts.md#in-screen-reader-mode).

Both apps remember whether screen reader mode is on.

## Known issues

- Opening a long book in screen reader mode takes a moment while the whole text is prepared.
- Windows screen reader mode has been tested with simulated keys, not yet with NVDA or JAWS themselves; reports are especially welcome.
- Tables in screen reader mode on Windows show each row on one line with cells divided by a bar; there is no cell-by-cell table navigation yet.
- Everything listed under [known issues in 0.3.0](0.3.0.md#known-issues) still applies.
