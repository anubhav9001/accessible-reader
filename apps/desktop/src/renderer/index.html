Reading commands

Every command is also in the menu bar; press Alt to reach it.

Books
CommandShortcutWhat it does
My booksCtrl+BLists every book you have opened or downloaded, most recently read first. Choose one to carry on reading, or remove it.
Open bookCtrl+OOpens an EPUB, a zipped DAISY book, or a DAISY folder's ncc.html or .opf file.
Close bookCtrl+WCloses the book, keeping your place, and goes back to My books.
Online librariesCtrl+LChoose Project Gutenberg or Sugamya Pustakalaya. Search or browse; Gutenberg books download and open in one step.
Reading modes
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Text onlyCtrl+1Shows the text. Play reads it aloud with the Windows voice.
Audio onlyCtrl+2Plays the recorded narration (or the Windows voice) with the text hidden.
Text and audioCtrl+3Plays the narration and highlights each phrase as it is read.
Faster / SlowerCtrl+] / Ctrl+[Changes the reading speed, from half speed to three times normal.
Playback and navigation
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Play or pauseCtrl+SpaceStarts or stops reading from the current phrase.
Next / previous phraseAlt+Right / Alt+LeftMoves one phrase or paragraph.
Next / previous headingAlt+Down / Alt+UpJumps to the next or previous chapter or section heading. In screen reader mode's text box, use H and Shift+H instead: Alt+Down and Alt+Up are left to your screen reader there.
Next / previous pageAlt+Page Down / Alt+Page UpMoves by printed page.
Next / previous sectionCtrl+Page Down / Ctrl+Page UpMoves to the next or previous section of the table of contents at the same level, skipping its sub-sections (for example, chapter to chapter). Next heading stops at every heading instead.
Go to pageCtrl+GGoes to a printed page number.
Go back / Go forwardBackspace / Shift+BackspaceAfter following a link or footnote, or jumping by the contents, a page number or a bookmark, goes back to where you were (and forward again). Works in the book text and the contents.
Add bookmarkCtrl+DBookmarks your place. Type a name or keep the suggested one (the start of the paragraph), then press Enter.
BookmarksCtrl+Shift+BLists this book's bookmarks in book order. Enter goes to one, F2 renames it, Delete removes it.
Heading depthSettings, or Navigate menuChooses which heading levels next and previous heading stop at.
DisplaySettings (Ctrl+,), DisplayHow the book's text looks: typeface, text size, bold, line, word and letter spacing, line length and margins, justified text, text and background colours (or your own), and hiding printed page numbers. Changes show at once and are remembered; Reset display settings goes back to the start.
eBraille: braille or print textCtrl+Shift+T, or View menueBraille books open as print text, translated back from braille, so they can be read aloud like any book. This switches to the braille itself (for a braille display) and back; Reader remembers the choice.
Enlarge a pictureClick the pictureOpens it as large as fits the window, with its description. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- make it larger and smaller, Ctrl+0 fits it to the window again; Escape closes it.
Moving around the window
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Table of contentsCtrl+TMoves to the contents list; choose an entry to go there.
Book textCtrl+EMoves to your place in the book text, for reading with your screen reader. Tabbing into the text lands there too.
Where am I?Ctrl+IOpens a window with your place (section, heading, page, how far through the book, time left), the reading mode and speed, and how long you have read this book. Escape closes it.
Book informationCtrl+Shift+IOpens a window with everything the book says about itself: title, authors, narrator, publisher, subject, description, format, accessibility, and every other metadata entry. Escape closes it.
SettingsCtrl+CommaReading speed, voice, heading depth, how much of the contents is shown open, text on the toolbar buttons, and screen reader mode.
Reading commandsF1Shows this list.
Screen reader mode
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Screen reader modeCtrl+Shift+RTurns it on or off (also the checkbox and the View menu). The book is shown as plain text. Read with the arrow keys and say all (say all may stop after a few pages; press it again); Reader remembers exactly where your cursor is, and puts it back there when you open the book again.
Next / previous headingH / Shift+HAnywhere in the book.
Heading at a level1 to 6 / Shift+1 to Shift+6Next or previous heading at that level.
Sentence. (full stop) / Shift+.Next or previous sentence, and reads it. Works with any screen reader, including Narrator.
PageP / Shift+PNext or previous printed page.
LinkK / Shift+KNext or previous link. Enter or Space follows a link inside the book; Backspace comes back.
TableT / Shift+TNext or previous table. A line before the table gives its size; each row is a line, cells divided by a bar and each cell starting with its column heading, then End of table. Enter on a table opens it as a real table, for your screen reader's table commands.
EquationM / Shift+MNext or previous equation (maths). Equations are read in words, in English or Hindi to match the book, for example "x equals the fraction with numerator …"; one on a line of its own starts with "Equation:". Enter on an equation opens it on its own, with its words and the equation as drawn.
List, list itemL / Shift+L, I / Shift+INext or previous list, or list item.
Image, figure, separatorG, F, S (Shift goes back)Next or previous image, figure or separator.
Section] / [Next or previous section (a part or chapter file of the book).
Where in the book=Says how far through the book you are, and the page.
Contents
CommandShortcutWhat it does
ContentsCtrl+T, then arrow keysUp and Down move between entries, Right and Left open and close a part, Home and End go to the first and last, a letter jumps to an entry starting with it, and Enter goes there. The entry you are reading is marked as current.

The reader remembers your place in each book, including where your screen reader read up to; in screen reader mode, down to the character. Play starts from there.
