Reading commands

Every command is also in the menu bar; press Alt to reach it.

Books
CommandShortcutWhat it does
My booksCtrl+BLists every book you have opened or downloaded, most recently read first. Choose one to carry on reading, or remove it.
Open bookCtrl+OOpens an EPUB, a zipped DAISY book, or a DAISY folder's ncc.html or .opf file.
Online librariesCtrl+LChoose Project Gutenberg or Sugamya Pustakalaya. Search or browse; Gutenberg books download and open in one step.
Reading modes
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Text onlyCtrl+1Shows the text. Play reads it aloud with the Windows voice.
Audio onlyCtrl+2Plays the recorded narration (or the Windows voice) with the text hidden.
Text and audioCtrl+3Plays the narration and highlights each phrase as it is read.
Faster / SlowerCtrl+] / Ctrl+[Changes the reading speed, from half speed to three times normal.
Playback and navigation
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Play or pauseCtrl+SpaceStarts or stops reading from the current phrase.
Next / previous phraseAlt+Right / Alt+LeftMoves one phrase or paragraph.
Next / previous headingAlt+Down / Alt+UpJumps to the next or previous chapter or section heading.
Next / previous pageAlt+Page Down / Alt+Page UpMoves by printed page.
Go to pageCtrl+GGoes to a printed page number.
Heading levelPlayback menuChooses which heading levels next and previous heading stop at.
Moving around the window
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Table of contentsCtrl+TMoves to the contents list; choose an entry to go there.
Book textCtrl+EMoves to your place in the book text, for reading with your screen reader. Tabbing into the text lands there too.
Where am I?Ctrl+IAnnounces the heading, page, time, mode and speed.
Reading commandsF1Shows this list.
Screen reader mode (like Paperback)
CommandShortcutWhat it does
Screen reader modeCtrl+Shift+RTurns it on or off (also the checkbox and the View menu). The whole book is shown as plain text in one read-only text box. Read with the arrow keys and say all; Reader remembers exactly where your cursor is, and puts it back there when you open the book again.
Next / previous headingH / Shift+HAnywhere in the book.
Heading at a level1 to 6 / Shift+1 to Shift+6Next or previous heading at that level.
PageP / Shift+PNext or previous printed page.
LinkK / Shift+KNext or previous link. Enter or Space follows a link inside the book.
TableT / Shift+TNext or previous table. Each row is a line, cells divided by a bar.
List, list itemL / Shift+L, I / Shift+INext or previous list, or list item.
Image, figure, separatorG, F, S (Shift goes back)Next or previous image, figure or separator.
Section] / [Next or previous section (a part or chapter file of the book).
Where in the book=Says how far through the book you are, and the page.
Contents
CommandShortcutWhat it does
ContentsCtrl+T, then arrow keysUp and Down move between entries, Right and Left open and close a part, Home and End go to the first and last, a letter jumps to an entry starting with it, and Enter goes there. The entry you are reading is marked as current.

The reader remembers your place in each book, down to the paragraph, including where your screen reader read up to. Play starts from there.
